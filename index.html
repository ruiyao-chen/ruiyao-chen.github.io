About

Ph.D. remote-sensing data scientist with 10+ years turning satellite and environmental data into validated products for NASA missions (TROPICS, GPM, TRMM, PolSIR). I specialize in the part most models skip: defining what "accurate" means for a product, designing the validation that proves it, and quantifying uncertainty for every prediction. I combine radiative-transfer physics with machine learning in Python, and I have taken models from research prototype to production code running in NASA's operational processing.

What I do

01Validation & uncertainty

Spatial and out-of-domain cross-validation, synthetic-truth tests, conformal prediction intervals, end-to-end error budgets. I find out how wrong a model is, where, and whether its stated uncertainty holds somewhere new.

02Physics + machine learning

Gradient boosting, CNNs and instance-based retrieval grounded in radiative-transfer simulation, so models stay physically interpretable and fail in ways you can predict.

03Models that ship

Reproducible, tested Python pipelines over multi-terabyte satellite archives, carried from prototype to production code delivered to NASA and improved from user feedback.
