Summary

Ph.D. remote-sensing data scientist with 10+ years turning satellite and environmental data into validated products for NASA missions (TROPICS, GPM, TRMM, PolSIR). I specialize in the part most models skip: defining what "accurate" means for a product, designing the validation that proves it, and quantifying uncertainty for every prediction. I combine radiative-transfer physics with machine learning in Python, and I have taken models from research prototype to production code running in NASA's operational processing.

Experience

Research Scientist

2023 – present

Vanderbilt University, Earth & Environmental Sciences · Nashville, TN / Princeton, NJ

  • Own NASA TROPICS' operational precipitation ice-water-path retrieval end to end: proposed the algorithm, built and validated it, wrote the production code and documentation, delivered it to NASA for operational processing, and improved later versions from user feedback.
  • Found brightness-temperature calibration drift between time periods in TROPICS Level-1 data that was propagating into retrievals; built per-period bias corrections from clear-sky radiative-transfer simulations, confirmed with the mission calibration team.
  • Built per-pixel uncertainty estimates for the ice retrieval and documented what they capture versus total error, so users read confidence correctly.
  • Built pre-launch simulated observations for NASA PolSIR (CRTM radiative transfer from ERA5 with per-channel noise, matched to ERA5, GEOS-5 and GOES-16/17), giving algorithm teams a known-truth testbed years before launch.

Postdoctoral Researcher

2018 – 2023

Vanderbilt University, Earth & Environmental Sciences · Nashville, TN

  • Co-PI and technical lead on a NASA GPM grant characterizing uncertainty in the multi-satellite precipitation record; built Monte Carlo uncertainty-quantification frameworks for precipitation and cloud retrievals.
  • Developed deep-learning (CNN with attention, MLP) and random-forest models for hydrometeor classification and rain-rate estimation from passive microwave data, grounded in radiative-transfer physics.
  • Built Python, Fortran and SQL pipelines to aggregate, clean and validate multi-terabyte satellite datasets.

Research Scientist

Environmental Model and Data Optima · Laurel, MD

Graduate Research Assistant

2012 – 2018

Central Florida Remote Sensing Lab, University of Central Florida · Orlando, FL

  • Root-caused two hidden on-orbit anomalies in NASA's 17-year TRMM Microwave Imager record and built physics-based corrections adopted in the TMI legacy (1B11 V8) reprocessing.
